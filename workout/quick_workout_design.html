🧠 Architectural Verdict: Total Exercise Lump Sum vs. Exercise Breakdown

Question: "Is it better to do quick workout and total number of exercise, or each exercise broken down?"

⚠️ Generic Total ("50 Reps Total")

Lumping reps into a single generic bucket gives you a streak day and total minutes, but breaks your PR leaderboard and milestones. The system cannot tell if you did 50 pushups or 50 jumping jacks, so pushup milestone badges, chest anatomy heatmaps, and movement PRs cannot trigger.

⭐ Winner: Fast Single-Click Movement Breakdown

Best of both worlds: Keep the user interaction dead-simple (default to Push-ups with 1-tap rep pills like +25), but under the hood save the canonical schema: { name: "Push-ups", category: "strength", discipline: "calisthenics", reps: 25 }. This instantly triggers Pushup PRs, unlocks Century Pushup Club badges, and lights up chest & triceps on the Anatomy map!
