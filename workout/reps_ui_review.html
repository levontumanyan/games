⚠️ Why the Collision Happened in the Screenshot:
  • Ring Container Shrinking: When media exists, .has-media .timer-ring-container shrinks the SVG ring to 110px × 110px.
  • Oversized Font Size: #timer-overlay.is-reps-stage #timer-display sets font-size: clamp(3.2rem, 8vw, 5.5rem). Fitting `5 REPS` inside 110px forces the number "5" to burst out the top over the GIF, while "REPS" wraps and clips into "RE".
  • DOM Button Injection: In player.js, doneBtn is injected inside .timer-center-text. Because .timer-center-text is vertically centered with transform: translate(-50%, -50%), adding the 48px button shifts the center downward, pushing the rep number upward across the border and on top of the GIF.
  • Circular Ring Irrelevance: Rep steps are target completions (e.g. "Do 5 Reps"), NOT timed countdowns. Circular progress rings take up vertical space for zero fitness value.

Root Cause Breakdown

Detailed Code Anatomy of the Bug

Here is exactly where the style and DOM conflict occurs in the codebase:

  1. Conflict 1: Shrunken Container vs Huge Font
    In styles.css:2759: .timer-stage-content.has-media .timer-ring-container { width: 110px; height: 110px; }.
    However, in styles.css:2789: #timer-overlay.is-reps-stage #timer-display { font-size: clamp(3.2rem, 8vw, 5.5rem); }.
    Because 3.5rem is ~56px tall and "5 REPS" is 6 characters wide, it physically cannot fit inside 110px and overflows outside the ring.
  2. Conflict 2: Button injected inside absolute centered text container
    In player.js:923: The Done button is appended to centerContainer (which is .timer-center-text).
    Since .timer-center-text is centered with transform: translate(-50%, -50%), adding the 48px button shifts the center point downward, pushing the rep number upward across the border and on top of the GIF!
