🧬 Human Anatomy Exercise Picker Explorations

Testing 4 interactive UX architectures where the human body diagram is the single source of truth for muscle targeting. Click between the 4 prototypes below and interact directly with the body models!

✨ Idea 1: Split Canvas with Target Mode Switcher (Recommended)

Direct side-by-side integration. The anatomy model is always visible right next to the exercise fields. A clean mode toggle lets you tap any muscle to instantly assign it as Primary Target (Red) or Secondary Synergist (Amber). The 34 static chips are completely eliminated in favor of live dynamic badges with instant ✕ remove buttons.

🎯 Mental Model: Point and paint ⚡ Clicks to pick: 1 tap per muscle 🧹 Clutter: Zero redundant chips ⚓ Pelvic Floor: Distinct sub-pubic & perineal basin

🔄 Idea 2: 1-Click State Cycle (Primary ➔ Secondary ➔ Off)

Zero mode toggle switches! Simply tap any muscle on the diagram. 1st click marks it as Primary Target (Red). Clicking the same muscle again promotes it to Secondary Synergist (Amber). A 3rd click turns it off. Fast and minimalist for touch screens.

🎯 Mental Model: Single-button state cycle ⚡ Clicks to pick: 1 tap for primary, 2 taps for secondary 🧹 Clutter: Minimal interface buttons
